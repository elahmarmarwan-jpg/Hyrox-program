No-sled swaps

Race stationDo this insteadHow
Sled pushTreadmill pushUse a curved, non-motorised treadmill if there is one. Otherwise a standard treadmill with the motor off, incline 3–5%, hands on the frame, short driving steps, body at 45°. Read distance off the display. If the gym won't allow it: bumper-plate push on a towel, or heavy goblet walking lunges for the same time.
Sled pullRope pullTie a battle rope to a heavy kettlebell or stacked plates, sit back in an athletic stance and pull hand-over-hand until it reaches you; walk it out and repeat to the distance. Fallback: heavy seated cable row ×15 + 20m backwards walk.
"Heavy"Effort, not kilosHeavy means slow, grinding steps you can only hold for 20–40s. Keep rest the same as written.
Before race day, try to book 2–3 sessions somewhere with real sleds at 152 kg / 103 kg, ideally for the Wk 10, 14 and 16 simulations.

Rules of the block

  • Missed a session? Drop Saturday first, then Wednesday. Protect Friday, and keep Thursday as rest.
  • Deload weeks 4, 8 and 12 are part of the plan. Don't add work back in.
  • Pick loads that hit the RPE shown; add 2.5–5 kg when every set feels easier than target.
  • Tests: baseline on kick-off day, retest Wk 4, half sims Wk 4 and Wk 8, full sims Wk 10, 14, 16 and 18.
  • If you fast in Ramadan: train after iftar, cut volume about 20%, keep Friday as the priority.
